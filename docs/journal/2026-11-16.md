# 2026-11-16

## Wheel button conflict and pedal lockup resolution (#242 / PR #256)

Resolved button conflict and pedal lockup when a racing wheel is connected alongside gamepads.
`WheelInput::prepare` now respects explicitly provided programmatic wheel configurations and avoids
unintended axis and button cross-talk when multiple devices are enumerated. Validated across all
8 fixture suites in `wheel_fixture_test`.

## Gamepad triggers deadzone, range, and curve modulation (#223)

Addressed player inquiries regarding touchy throttle/brake response and perceived early saturation
(reaching 100% at 60–70% physical trigger travel) on modern gamepad analog triggers (LT/RT):
- Updated `docs/CONTROLS.md` with a troubleshooting and configuration section detailing how GT4 vehicle
  physics and engine torque curves interact with linear trigger input.
- Documented launcher controls settings: selecting `Quadratic` or `Smooth Power (x^1.5)` curves to dampen
  low-to-mid travel and enable progressive throttle feathering through corners without wheelspin.
- Documented `Full trigger range` (`trigger_full_range`) behavior (active by default since rc14),
  ensuring all 255 steps are utilized rather than the legacy 46/85-step DualShock 2 scale.
