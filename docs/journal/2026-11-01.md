# 2026-11-01

## TASK-140: Hood camera wind noise and 480p full color range study (Issue #135)

- Investigated user report in GitHub Issue #135 (`shadowcla02-cloud`) regarding:
  1. Excessive aerodynamic wind rush audio in bonnet/hood camera perspective at high driving speeds.
  2. Perceived color gamut/range and gradient stepping/banding in 480p progressive mode compared to 480i interlaced mode.
- Completed comprehensive engineering study and architecture document: `docs/TASK-140-HOOD-WIND-480P-COLOR-STUDY.md`.
- Technical findings:
  - **Bonnet Cam Wind Audio:** GT4\'s `RaceCarSound` module dynamically scales a dedicated SPU2 looping air rush voice based on forward velocity $v_{\text{car}}$ and camera perspective weight ($K_{\text{camera}} \approx 1.0$ for hood/bumper vs $0.25$–$0.35$ for chase cam). Outlined EE instruction/RAM gain clamping hook (`GT4_BONNET_WIND_VOLUME=0.0..1.0`) to mute or attenuate the wind sound without degrading mechanical car audio (engine, transmission, tire grip).
  - **480p Color Degradation:** Identified hardware root cause from PS2 GS VRAM architecture. In 480i field rendering, the game uses PSMCT24 (24-bit RGB888, 16.7M colors, 8 bits/channel) for two $640 \times 224$ buffers (`0x69000` words). In 480p progressive mode, the resolution doubles to $640 \times 448$, forcing Polyphony Digital to halve color depth to PSMCT16 (16-bit RGB5551, 32K colors, 5 bits/channel) to fit inside the identical `0x69000`-word footprint. On modern LCD/OLED monitors, 5-bit color stepping causes noticeable banding and posterization. Outlined 480p PSMCT32 mode upgrade (`psm = 1`, `0x8C000` words), Vulkan swapchain full-range tone curve alignment, and host post-processing deband filter options.
