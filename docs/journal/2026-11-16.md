# 2026-11-16

## Wheel button conflict and pedal lockup resolution (issue #242; TASK-174)

Player report (issue #242): on 1.3.0 rc 16, XO^D face buttons stop working on wheel or gamepad when a wheel is connected.
Root cause identified and resolved:
1. When a wheel with normal-polarity pedals (G920, Direct Drive, load-cells) was connected under the generic preset (which assumes inverted pedals), pedals resting at -32768 evaluated to 100% full press (255) on throttle and brake. In addition, uninitialized axes sitting at 0 in SDL evaluated to 50% press (128).
2. Under `PedalMapMode::cross_square`, throttle and brake continuously asserted Cross (bit 14) and Square (bit 15) on the DualShock 2 PadState, preventing edge detection in menus and blocking other face buttons (Circle and Triangle).
3. In DFP mode, `prepared_pad_` was never populated, starving DualShock 2 of wheel button input in menus.
4. Logitech G920 had no dedicated preset and defaulted to generic with inverted pedals and swapped button indices.

Changes made:
- Added `logitech_g920` preset with normal-polarity pedals and correct Xbox button mapping.
- Implemented `detect_preset_for_device` to automatically match known wheels (G920, G29, Thrustmaster, Moza, Fanatec, HORI) when preset is generic or auto.
- Protected uninitialized pedal axes so resting values (+32767 for inverted, -32768 for normal) are used until first hardware movement.
- Populated digital buttons on `prepared_pad_` in DFP mode for seamless menu navigation.
- Added comprehensive unit test suite in `native/wheel_fixture.cpp` (`test_wheel_button_conflict_and_detection`), passing 100%.
